Ardena and Ardena Pay are built to work together — from booking a car to completing the payment, the experience is seamless.

Ardena

Africa's peer-to-peer car rental platform. Ardena connects car owners (hosts) with people who need a vehicle (clients) — making car rental accessible, affordable, and community-powered across Africa.

Visit us at ardena.co.ke

Host App Client App
Host App

Car owners list their vehicles, set availability, pricing, and receive bookings. Full control, maximum earnings.

Client App

Browse available vehicles, book instantly, track your rental, and pay — all in one seamless experience.

Real-time tracking

Live GPS tracking for every rental so both hosts and renters always know where the vehicle is.

Verified community

Identity verification, reviews, and ratings build trust between hosts and clients on every booking.

Ardena Pay

Ardena Pay is a Stellar-powered digital wallet built to handle payments within the Ardena ecosystem. Currently in beta, it enables renters to pay for car rentals directly through the Ardena platform — fast, borderless, and built on blockchain infrastructure.

Powered by Stellar blockchain
Stellar blockchain settlement

Transactions settle on the Stellar network — fast, low-cost, and auditable on a public blockchain.

Rental payments

Renters pay for their Ardena bookings through Ardena Pay — no need for cash or traditional card processors.

Fast cross-border settlement

Stellar's global network means payments settle in seconds regardless of currency or country.

Currently in beta

Ardena Pay is actively being tested with early users in the Ardena rental ecosystem. Full launch coming soon.
