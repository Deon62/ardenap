How we work.

Three platforms out of one small team isn't an accident. It comes from keeping the loop between deciding and shipping as short as possible.

Work with us What we build

Builders talk to customers

The engineer who wrote the feature is in the conversation with the host, organiser or client using it. Nothing gets lost in translation.

Ship, then refine

We would rather have something real in a user's hands this month than something perfect next year. Every one of our products started small.

Own it end to end

Whoever builds a thing owns it in production: the bugs, the support questions, the 2am alert. It's the fastest way to learn what quality means.
