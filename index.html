Ardena is a peer-to-peer car rental platform that connects car owners (hosts) with people who need a vehicle (clients). Hosts list their cars, clients browse and book — all through two dedicated apps built for the African market. Visit ardena.co.ke to get started.

Download the Ardena Host App, create an account, and list your vehicle with photos, availability, and pricing. You're in full control — approve bookings, set your own rates, and manage your calendar. Payments are received securely through Ardena Pay.

Ardena Pay is our digital payment layer, powered by the Stellar blockchain. It enables renters to pay for car rentals seamlessly within the Ardena platform. Ardena Pay is currently in beta — offering fast, secure, and borderless settlement for every transaction on the platform.

Ardena is currently rolling out across African cities. Check ardena.co.ke for the latest coverage. If your city is not yet listed, you can sign up to be notified when we expand to your area — or list your car early to help grow the community there.

Every host and client goes through identity verification before they can transact on the platform. Both parties leave reviews after each rental, building a transparent reputation system. Payments are held securely and only released once the rental is confirmed, protecting both hosts and clients.
