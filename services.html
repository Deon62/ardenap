Software development

From a first prototype to a platform with thousands of users. We design, build and ship web applications, Android and iOS apps, and the APIs, databases and admin tools that keep them running, documented so your team can own them after handover.

What's included

  • 01Web platforms, portals & dashboards
  • 02Android & iOS apps, through store release
  • 03APIs, backends & admin panels
  • 04Payment & M-Pesa integrations
  • 05Maintenance, hosting & support

Proof: Ardena and Tickopedia run on web, Android and iOS, built and published by us.

AI solutions

We put AI to work where it pays for itself: assistants that answer from your documents and cite their source, extraction that turns PDFs and forms into structured data, and AI features inside the products you already run. Every system is evaluated before launch and built so your data stays yours.

What's included

  • 01Assistants grounded in your documents
  • 02Customer support on web & WhatsApp
  • 03Document reading & data extraction
  • 04AI features in existing products
  • 05Evaluation, guardrails & data privacy

Proof: als answers students from their own notes, with every answer cited to the page.

Business automation

Most businesses lose hours every week to copy-paste between systems, manual reconciliation and reports built by hand. We map those processes, then connect your tools and automate them, with clear logs and alerts so nothing fails quietly.

What's included

  • 01Workflow & approval automation
  • 02System integrations (CRM, ERP, accounting)
  • 03M-Pesa & bank reconciliation
  • 04Automated reports & dashboards
  • 05SMS, email & WhatsApp notifications

For example: the weekly report that takes someone a full day, running on a schedule instead.

Technology consultancy

Independent advice from people who build and run production systems. We help you decide what to build, buy or skip; review architecture and code; scope products into realistic plans and budgets; and prepare for data-protection obligations under Kenyan law.

What's included

  • 01Technology strategy & roadmaps
  • 02Product scoping & MVP planning
  • 03Architecture & code reviews
  • 04Build vs. buy & vendor selection
  • 05Data protection & security readiness

Starts with a short discovery sprint and a written recommendation you keep.

Digital commerce

What's included

Payments & fintech integration

We connect your product to the payment rails customers in Africa actually use (M-Pesa, cards and bank transfers) and build the money flows around them: checkout, payouts to vendors or hosts, refunds, wallets and the reconciliation that keeps finance happy.

What's included

  • 01M-Pesa STK Push, C2B & B2C
  • 02Card & bank payment gateways
  • 03Payouts, splits & commissions
  • 04Wallets, invoicing & subscriptions
  • 05Reconciliation & payment reporting

Proof: Ardena and Tickopedia take payments and handle bookings end to end.
