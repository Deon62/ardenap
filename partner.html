Who we
partner with.

Our platforms are marketplaces, they only work when the supply side is strong. These are the partners we're actively looking for, and what each one brings to the table.

01

Hosts & fleet operators

The people with the cars. Individual owners with one idle vehicle, fleet operators, dealerships, leasing companies and corporate fleets: all of you can list on Ardena and reach pre-vetted renters.

Whether you manage one vehicle or five hundred, you get listing tools, availability control, your own pricing, GPS tracking and verified renters, with no upfront investment in a platform of your own.

Individual hosts Fleet operators Dealerships Leasing Corporate fleets
02

Insurance companies

Peer-to-peer rental needs cover built for it. We want insurers who can underwrite short-term, host-to-renter policies embedded directly into the booking flow, priced at the point of transaction, with no friction for either side and clean trip-level data on our end.

Motor insurance P2P cover Embedded insurance Underwriters
03

Event organisers & venues

Promoters, venues, festivals, conference organisers and university societies: publish on Tickopedia, sell secure digital tickets, message your attendees and check them in by QR at the door. If you run events regularly, we'll onboard you directly.

Promoters Venues Festivals Conferences
04

Businesses in our line of work

Banks, microfinance providers and fintechs extending vehicle financing or rental credit. Schools and universities that want als in front of their students. Agencies and companies that need a website, an app or a backend built, and would rather work with a team that ships its own products than one that only ships slide decks.

Banks & fintech Microfinance Schools & universities Software clients

What a partnership
actually gives you.

No equity, no long lock-ins, no vague memoranda of understanding. Commercial agreements with a defined scope, and a working platform on day one.

Distribution from day one

Our platforms are live on the web and on both app stores. You plug into demand that already exists instead of building an audience from scratch.

Real technical integration

APIs, webhooks and embedded flows built by the same engineers who built the platform, not a referral link and a logo on a page.

Verified users on both sides

Identity verification, reviews and ratings are built in. Every host, renter and organiser you meet through us has been through it.

A direct line to the builders

You talk to the people who write the code. Small team, short chain, decisions in days rather than quarters.
