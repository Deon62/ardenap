We build technology
Africa deserves.

A software and product company founded in Nakuru in 2026, building our own platforms across mobility, education and live events, and building software for the businesses around us.

Africa-first,
always.

Africa has long been on the receiving end of technology built for other markets: adapted, retrofitted, and never quite right. Ardena exists to change that.

Founded on March 17, 2026, we set out to create technology products designed specifically for Africa's infrastructure realities, its regulatory landscape, its cross-border economy, and most importantly, its people.

That works in two directions. We build and run our own platforms, Ardena for mobility, als for education, Tickopedia for live events. And we put the same team to work building websites, apps and software for other businesses who want it done properly.

Our values.

01

Africa-native thinking

Every product decision starts with Africa's realities, not global averages. We design for local payment methods, local connectivity, and diverse regulatory contexts.

02

Radical simplicity

The best technology disappears. We obsess over removing friction so that our products feel invisible, not complicated or foreign.

03

Trust by default

Security, compliance, and user privacy are foundational requirements, not afterthoughts. On als, for instance, a student's coursework is never used to train models.

04

We ship what we sell

Three products live on the web and on both app stores. When we tell a client we can build something, it's because we've already built one for ourselves.
